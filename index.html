Immigration Notes

Goiás attracts internal immigrants from Northeast for agribusiness jobs. International for specialized roles. Immigration cases include skilled worker visas for mining. Challenges include rural isolation. Opportunities in expanding agriculture sector.

Common Applicant Profiles

  • eco-tourists
  • family from Northeast
  • business investors

Economic Drivers

  • soy and corn agriculture

  • tourism (national parks)
  • food processing

What are the main economic opportunities in Goiás?

What local challenges might foreigners face in Goiás?
